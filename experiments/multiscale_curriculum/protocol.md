# Fixed protocol: multiscale TCN, curriculum, and conditional weights

Forecast eight consecutive 15-minute blocks over the next 120 clock minutes. Each block has three targets: mean M1 Close, minimum M1 Low, maximum M1 High. Predict the conditional 10th, 50th and 90th quantiles of each statistic. These are marginal prediction intervals, not coherent sampled future scenarios or guaranteed calibrated bounds.

Inputs: 360 M1 minutes, 576 completed M5 buckets, 336 completed H1 buckets (calendar windows, including closures). Treat source timestamps as candle starts. Forecast origin is the end of the last visible M1 bar. Exclude unfinished aggregate buckets. Missing minute candles carry the last known Close, with an observed-fraction feature. Future target periods must have all 120 real M1 observations. One-hour-dollar scale uses only close changes within the last 360 visible minutes, floored at the 5th percentile of TRAIN scales. The scale is also an explicit input feature. Price levels are centered at the last visible Close, divided by scale, and asinh-compressed. Include daily clock sine/cosine; no future inputs.

Training: January 2024, every eligible M1 origin, 26,045 examples. Validation: February 1–7, origins every 15 minutes, 397 examples. Test: February 15–29, origins every 15 minutes, 856 examples. Target horizons cannot cross their split end. Test was moved outside the previous short experiment's February 8–14 window. Overlapping origins are correlated; do not treat them as independent observations.

Architecture: three independent TCN towers, width 64; each has six causal residual blocks with two kernel-three convolutions, dilations 1,2,4,8,16,32, LayerNorm, SiLU and dropout 25%. Patch sizes 4 M1, 6 M5, 4 H1. Concatenate final and mean features of each tower; project to context dimension 192. The hypernetwork generates a rank-four correction A(c)B(c)/2 to a 128→64 head layer. Static head width 191 matches hypernetwork capacity within 0.1%. All shared features and generated weights learn jointly. Ordered output quantiles and sorted low/mean/high outputs enforce valid ordering.

Four stages: (1) describe four 30-minute blocks of the visible past using Huber loss, cap 3 epochs; (2) future 60/120-minute range and total absolute Close changes using quantile pinball loss, cap 4; (3) four future 30-minute mean prices, pinball + 0.1 activity loss, cap 4; (4) eight future 15-minute mean/min/max targets, pinball + 0.1 coarse + 0.1 activity, cap 9. Stage 1 establishes a deterministic representation task, not evidence of forecasting skill. Transfer the lowest primary validation-loss checkpoint and its optimizer state between stages. Prediction heads start at TRAIN marginal quantiles (past head at TRAIN medians).

Compare architecture {static,hyper} × training {curriculum,direct} × seed {17,29}: eight fits. The direct model starts fresh and trains stage 4 only, with an epoch cap equal to its paired curriculum's actual total epochs. Both have global-epoch LR 2e-4 for epochs 1–6, 1e-4 for 7–12, 5e-5 thereafter. AdamW, weight decay 1e-4, batch 128, clipping norm 2. Each epoch traverses all training examples chronologically once. Early stopping: 3 epochs without 0.5% relative primary validation improvement; minimum stage epochs 3,3,3,4 (direct:6). Direct may stop earlier. This matches an upper bound on data passes, not exact achieved compute or optimizer trajectories. Report actual budgets.

Epoch choice and family choice use normalized pinball loss on validation. Ensemble two seeds by averaging predicted quantiles (an ensemble summary, not an exact mixture quantile). Select family before reading test results. Quantile loss is a proper per-target objective; evaluate both normalized and dollar errors, interval coverage and width, central path error per horizon.

Baselines: empirical TRAIN quantiles of normalized future block statistics, scaled using the same past-only test volatility; current-Close persistence for mean-price MAE. Hypernetwork diagnostic: replace conditioning with mean TRAIN context at selected weights, without retraining. Not eligible for family selection.

Audits: independent raw OHLC target reconstruction and aggregate availability; quantile ordering; deterministic eval, stochastic dropout; generator/trunk gradients; paired initial trunks; matched capacity; full-epoch example counts. Save stage/best weights, losses, predictions and source/data hashes. No test-driven hyperparameter changes. Worker budget 50 minutes. Stop cloud GPU after downloading final artifacts.
