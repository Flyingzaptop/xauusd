## New causal MBO experiment

Only **past** M1 and MBO are visible. Predict next M1 H/C/L angles; approximate Open by previous Close. Slow context: 512 M1 angular tokens. Fast context: up to 2048 individual MBO events within the last 30 minutes, strictly `ts_recv < target minute start`. GC futures are a different instrument/source from XAUUSD.

- Train: 2025-10-01 through 2026-05-31; validation: June 2026; test: July 1–30, 2026.
- Eligible samples: 132,366 train; 17,598 validation; 17,588 test.
- Verification passed for 167,552 total cached windows; independent raw-DBN comparison and GC-bar resampling passed.
- Six causal/model tests passed; all three variants completed GPU smoke training.
- Controls: M1 only; M1 + GC trade-price history; M1 + GC price history + raw MBO.
- Initial LR 1e-4, dropout .25, AdamW weight decay .01. Complete chronological epochs, no target subsampling. Maximum 30 epochs, validation stopping after five non-improving epochs.

The first terminal-based launch stopped before completing its first epoch; its stale progress is preserved in `interrupted-progress.json`. No completed training checkpoint or final July metrics were produced by that launch. On 2026-10-09 the agent restarted training while preparing publication, then stopped both pipeline and trainer at the user's explicit request. The automatic restart was not requested by the user. Do not resume training automatically. `pipeline.json` records `stopped_by_user`.

The experiment's artifact files are a point-in-time snapshot. Partial training progress and smoke metrics are not final held-out results. No final July performance is claimed. Source hashes in the original cache verification describe the earlier code version; the publication inventory hashes describe the current launcher version.
